Do not use for the preservation of life or property. Satellite-derived active fire / thermal anomalies have limited accuracy.\
Please see our [Disclaimer](https://www.earthdata.nasa.gov/data/projects/lance#ed-firms-citation)

Active fire/thermal anomalies may be from fire, hot smoke, agriculture or other sources.

Cloud cover may obscure active fire detections.

[What does a fire detection mean on the ground?](https://www.earthdata.nasa.gov/data/tools/firms/faq)

[Frequently Asked Questions](https://www.earthdata.nasa.gov/data/tools/firms/faq)

#### Additional information

Red squares show satellite derived thermal anomalies which may be from fire, hot smoke, agriculture or other sources.

The size of the red squares represent an approximate 1km x 1km pixel for MODIS data and 375m x 375m pixel for VIIRS.

Detected activity could be a small, intense fire or a fire burning over a broader area within the pixel. It does not mean the entire square is entirely on fire.

[What does a fire detection mean on the ground?](https://www.earthdata.nasa.gov/data/tools/firms/faq)

The spatial resolution, satellite view geometry and detection confidence available as attributes for each detection/hotspot should be considered when using these data.

Due to their relatively coarse spatial resolution and technical considerations inherent to satellite-derived data, they should be applied carefully at relatively local scales.
