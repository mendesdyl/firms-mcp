For more information on the active fire product and other MODIS fire products, please refer to the [FIRMS publications](https://www.earthdata.nasa.gov/data/tools/firms) by Louis Giglio as well as the [MODIS Active Fire Burned Area Products](http://modis-fire.umd.edu/) website and the [MODIS Burned Area User Guide (Updated November 2022)](https://modis-fire.umd.edu/files/MODIS_C6_BA_User_Guide_1.3.pdf) and the [MODIS Collection 6 Active Fire Product User Guide (Updated December 2020)](https://modis-fire.umd.edu/files/MODIS_C61_BA_User_Guide_1.1.pdf).
