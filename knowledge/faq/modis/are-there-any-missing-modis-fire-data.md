Terra was launched on December 18, 1999, and Aqua was launched on May 4, 2002. High quality active fire observations are available from the Terra satellite starting November 2000 and from the Aqua satellite starting July 4, 2002, onwards.

In the fire data archive, there are several days where data were not collected and days with lower than usual fire counts due to reasons such as instrument outage. These include, but are not limited to: April 15, 2001; June 15 to July 3, 2001; and March 19 to 28, 2002.

To check if there are any days where data is missing from the MODIS instrument, please refer to the [FIRMS missing data table](https://firms.modaps.eosdis.nasa.gov/api/missing_data/). This page shows the days in which there are no data for a particular satellite and instrument combination. This information is provided for both standard quality data and near real-time (NRT) data.
