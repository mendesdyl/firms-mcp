The Moderate Resolution Imaging Spectroradiometer (MODIS) is a payload imaging sensor that was launched into Earth orbit by NASA in 1999 aboard the Terra (EOS AM) satellite and in 2002 aboard the Aqua (EOS PM) satellite.

MODIS reflectance imagery that corresponds to the active fire detections, can be viewed in [FIRMS Global](https://firms.modaps.eosdis.nasa.gov/map/), [FIRMS US/Canada](https://firms.modaps.eosdis.nasa.gov/usfs/map/) and [Worldview](https://worldview.earthdata.nasa.gov/?p=geographic&l=VIIRS_SNPP_CorrectedReflectance_TrueColor(hidden),MODIS_Aqua_CorrectedReflectance_TrueColor(hidden),MODIS_Terra_CorrectedReflectance_TrueColor,VIIRS_SNPP_Fires_375m_Day(hidden),VIIRS_SNPP_Fires_375m_Night(hidden),MODIS_Fires_All,Reference_Labels(hidden),Reference_Features(hidden),Coastlines&v=-96.5390625,-61.171875,105.2578125,67.640625).
