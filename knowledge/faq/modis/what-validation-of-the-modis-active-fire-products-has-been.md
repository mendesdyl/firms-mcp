Validation of the Terra MODIS Fire Product has primarily been performed using coincident observations from the [Advanced Spaceborne Thermal Emission and Reflection Radiometer (ASTER)](https://asterweb.jpl.nasa.gov/); see the [MODIS Land Team Validation](http://landval.gsfc.nasa.gov/ProductStatus.php?ProductID=MOD14) page, and see [FIRMS publications](https://www.earthdata.nasa.gov/data/tools/firms) by Csiszar, et al. (2006) and two publications from Morisette, et al. (2005) for details. A very brief discussion of the general validation procedure, with some preliminary results, can be found in the Justice, et al. (2002) publication.
