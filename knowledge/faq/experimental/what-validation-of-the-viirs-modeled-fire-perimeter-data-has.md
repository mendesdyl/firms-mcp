[Chen et al., 2022](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41597-022-01343-0) provide a rigorous validation of the FEDS algorithm for large fire events in California between 2012-2020. The study compared the final FEDS perimeter data to official year-end fire perimeter data from the Fire and Resource Assessment Program (FRAP), established by the California Department of Forestry and Fire Protection. [Table 6 in Chen et al. (2022)](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41597-022-01343-0/tables/7) summarizes the comparison between FEDS and FRAP data for large fires in California in 2018 using standard comparison metrics (e.g., Accuracy, Precision, Recall, and Intersection Over Union). Overall, the FEDS data compare favorably to the FRAP year-end data. Validation for other regions and other fire types is an area of ongoing research by the EIS Fire Project team, and updates from these ongoing studies will be added, as available.
