The VIIRS Modeled Fire Perimeter data based on the FEDS algorithm are updated twice per day using the VIIRS 375 m active fire information from the morning (01:30) and afternoon (13:30) Suomi-NPP and NOAA-20 satellite overpasses over each fire event. The vector output from the FEDS algorithm is produced within approximately 4 hours following the availability of VIIRS 375 m active fire data in FIRMS.
