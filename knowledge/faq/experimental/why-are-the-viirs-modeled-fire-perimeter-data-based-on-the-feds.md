The FEDS algorithm was originally developed and tested for large fires in California ([Chen et al., 2022](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41597-022-01343-0)). The EIS Fire team has scaled the production of the fire event tracking approach to cover CONUS and Canada, with data available via [OGC API](https://firenrt.delta-backend.com/). Data for additional regions, including Alaska and Hawaii, will be released through FIRMS when available.
