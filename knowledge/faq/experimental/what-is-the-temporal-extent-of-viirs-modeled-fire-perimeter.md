The VIIRS Modeled Fire Perimeter data based on the FEDS algorithm are currently available through FIRMS beginning January 1, 2024. Archive data for prior years is available via [OGC API](https://firenrt.delta-backend.com/). This example notebook, [Get Fire Perimeters from an OGC API](https://nasa-impact.github.io/veda-docs/notebooks/tutorials/mapping-fires.html), demonstrates how to subset, analyze, and visualize these data outside of FIRMS.
