The VIIRS Modeled Fire Perimeter data based on the FEDS algorithm are provided by NASA's Earth Information System ([EIS](https://www.earthdata.nasa.gov/data/projects/eis)) Project, which harnesses the full power of NASA’s satellite observations, models, and scientific expertise to deliver information for societal benefit that is open, accessible, and actionable. The FEDS algorithm was developed by Yang Chen and colleagues at the University of California-Irvine, NASA’s Goddard Space Flight Center (GSFC), Cardiff University (UK), and Universidad del Rosario (Colombia), as described in a scientific paper published in 2022 ([Chen et al., 2022](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41597-022-01343-0)).

Reference:

Chen Y, Hantson S, Andela N, Coffield SR, Graff CA, Morton DC, Ott LE, Foufoula-Georgio E, Smyth P, Goulden M, Randerson JT. 2022. California wildfire spread derived using VIIRS satellite observations and an object-based tracking system. *Scientific Data*, 9: 249.
