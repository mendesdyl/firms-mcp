Depending on the instrument and the satellite's orbit, the swaths from consecutive orbits might not completely overlap at the equator. This results in small, diamond-shaped gaps in daily coverage in tropical regions (between approximately 30 degrees North and South). However, these gaps are typically covered on the satellite's next pass or by a different satellite in the FIRMS network.
