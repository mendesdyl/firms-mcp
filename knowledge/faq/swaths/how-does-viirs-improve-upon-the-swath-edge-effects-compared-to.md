The VIIRS instrument features onboard processing that limits the growth of pixels toward the edge of the swath. While MODIS pixels expand significantly at swath edges, VIIRS maintains a much more consistent pixel size across its entire 3,040 km field of view. This means VIIRS provides higher spatial resolution and more consistent active fire detection capability, even at the extreme edges of its observational swath.
