A swath is the strip of Earth's surface that satellite instruments (like MODIS or VIIRS) observe or "see" as they orbit the planet. You can think of it as the footprint of the sensor's vision. As the satellite moves along its orbital path, the instrument images the strip below the satellite, collecting data that is used to generate imagery and detect thermal anomalies and active fires.
