Yes. The spatial resolution (pixel size) is best directly under the satellite, known as the "nadir." For example, a MODIS pixel is 1 km x 1 km at nadir. However, towards the edges of the swath, the sensor is viewing Earth at an angle, which causes the pixel to stretch and cover a larger area on the ground (often referred to as the "bow-tie effect"). While a fire will still be detected at the edge of a swath, the exact location and the minimum detectable fire size may be less precise compared to a detection at nadir.
