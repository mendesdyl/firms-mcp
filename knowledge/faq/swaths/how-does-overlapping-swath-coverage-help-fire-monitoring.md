Because Earth is rotating while a satellite orbits (see this [Aqua MODIS animation](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d4QLDlAumOc)), the swaths from consecutive orbits often overlap, especially at high latitudes. Furthermore, by using multiple satellites (such as Terra and Aqua for MODIS, or Suomi NPP and NOAA-20 for VIIRS), FIRMS can provide multiple swaths over the same area at different times of day. This frequent revisit time is crucial for tracking the progression of active fires and monitoring diurnal fire behavior.
