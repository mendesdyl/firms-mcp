The width of the swath determines how much of Earth's surface can be monitored in a single pass. Instruments used in FIRMS typically have very wide swaths (e.g., about 2,330 km for MODIS and 3,040 km for VIIRS). This wide coverage allows these satellites to image almost the entire globe at least twice a day, enabling new, rapidly spreading fires to be detected.
