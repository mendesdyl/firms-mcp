Landsat provides better active fire detection performance compared to coarser spatial resolution sensors, such as MODIS and VIIRS. Flaming fires as small as approximately 4 m<sup>2</sup> have a better than 50% chance of detection during the daytime observations. Nighttime detection of similar fire activity improves to an area as small as 1 m<sup>2</sup>.
