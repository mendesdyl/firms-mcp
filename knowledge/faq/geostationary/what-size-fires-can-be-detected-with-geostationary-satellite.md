GOES - The theoretical minimum detectable fire size is approximately 0.004 km<sup>2</sup> (4,000m<sup>2</sup>) at nadir in optimum viewing conditions, burning at 800 K (Schmidt et al., 2013).

Meteosat - The theoretical minimum detectable fire size for “actively burning fires” is approximately 0.0001 of the area of a 3km SEVIRI pixel at nadir or approximately 0.0009km<sup>2</sup> (900m<sup>2</sup>) (Schmidt et al., 2013).

Himawari - Documentation describing the minimum detectable fire size of Himawari AHI using JAXA's [Wild Fire product algorithm](https://www.eorc.jaxa.jp/ptree/documents/README_H08_L2WLF.txt) is currently limited but performance is anticipated to be similar to GOES ABI. [Several recent research efforts](https://scholar.google.com/scholar?hl=en&as_sdt=0%2C45&q=himawari+8+wildfire+algorithm&btnG=) demonstrate the use of Himawari AHI using customized/adapted fire detection algorithms with varying results.
