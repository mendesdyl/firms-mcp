- GOES active fire detections occur every 10 minutes and should be available within FIRMS in approximately 20 to 30 minutes.
- Meteosat Third Generation (MTG-0) active fire detections occur every 10 minutes and should be available within FIRMS in approximately 20 to 30 minutes.
- Meteosat Second Generation (e.g. MSG 0 and MSG IODC) active fire detections occur every 15 minutes and should be available within FIRMS in approximately 30 minutes.
- Himawari active fire detections occur every 10 minutes and should be available within FIRMS in approximately 30 minutes.
