The Forecasted Fire Danger layer is based on the [National Fire Danger Rating System (NFDRS)](https://www.fs.usda.gov/detail/scnfs/home/?cid=fseprd634957), which produces several components and indexes (e.g., ignition probability, fire rate of spread, etc.) that collectively describe current and potential fire danger conditions. Daily forecasted fire danger updates are used by wildland fire management agencies to assess current fire danger at local and national levels and inform decisions on an area’s fire protection needs. NFDRS fire danger is expressed as an [adjective class rating](https://www.wfas.net/index.php/fire-danger-rating-fire-potential--danger-32/class-rating-fire-potential-danger-51?task=view) that normalizes rating classes across different fuel models, indexes, and station locations.
