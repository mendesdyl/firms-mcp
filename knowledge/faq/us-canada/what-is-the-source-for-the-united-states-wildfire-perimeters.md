The US Fire Perimeter layer displays the extent of current wildfires based on perimeter data from the [National Incident Feature Service (NIFS)](https://data-nifc.opendata.arcgis.com/datasets/5da472c6d27b4b67970acc7b5044c862_0) maintained by the [National Interagency Fire Center (NIFC)](https://www.nifc.gov/fire-information). Incident management teams for individual wildfire incidents are the source for these data. Input data/information sources for routine perimeter updates conducted by incident management teams, as well as the update frequency, can vary.
