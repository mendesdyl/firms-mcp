Additional information about current large wildfires in the United States can be found in the [Incident Management Situation Report](https://www.nifc.gov/nicc/sitreprt.pdf) published by the [National Interagency Coordination Center](https://www.nifc.gov/nicc/). Additional information about current large wildfires in Canada can be found in the [National Fire Situation Report](https://ciffc.net/en/ciffc/ext/public/sitrep/) published by the [Canadian Interagency Forest Fire Center (CIFFC)](https://ciffc.net/en).
