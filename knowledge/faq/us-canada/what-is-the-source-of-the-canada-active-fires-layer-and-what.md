The Canada Active Fires layer is based on information from the Canadian Wildland Fire Information System [Active Wildfires](https://cwfis.cfs.nrcan.gc.ca/datamart/metadata/activefires) dataset coordinated and maintained by the [Canadian Interagency Forest Fire Center (CIFFC)](https://ciffc.net/) and [Natural Resources Canada](https://www.nrcan.gc.ca/home). Reported wildfire locations are updated and provided daily by partner provincial and territorial fire management agencies and Parks Canada. The Canada Active Fires layer displays the location of current wildfires greater than 100 acres (40 hectares) in size and selected descriptive information, including fire name/ID, latest reported size, and date/time of ignition.
