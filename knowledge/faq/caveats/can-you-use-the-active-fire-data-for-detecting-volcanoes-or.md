While both the MODIS and VIIRS algorithms routinely detect active volcanoes, the active fire products have not been validated against independent data for their ability to detect volcanoes. There is a separate near-real time MODIS product, [MODVOLC](http://modis.higp.hawaii.edu/), specifically for volcanoes.
