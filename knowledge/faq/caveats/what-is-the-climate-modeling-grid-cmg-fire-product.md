The CMG fire products are gridded statistical summaries of fire pixel information intended for use in regional and global modeling. The products are currently generated at 0.5 degree spatial resolution for time periods of 1 calendar month (MOD14CMH/MYD14CMH) and 8 days (MOD14C8H/MYD14C8H). Higher resolution 0.25 degree CMG fire products will eventually be produced as well. More information can be found in the [MODIS Collection 6 Active Fire Product User's Guide](https://modis-fire.umd.edu/files/MODIS_C6_Fire_User_Guide_C.pdf).
