Because HLS data relies on optical imagery from the Landsat and Sentinel-2 satellites, it cannot see through clouds. Cloud cover presents a limitation when analyzing land surfaces because they will obscure the view of the ground and vegetation. However, by combining the data streams from multiple satellites, HLS provides a higher temporal frequency (plot-scale observations every 2 to 4 days). This combined rapid revisit time significantly increases the chances of capturing clear-sky, cloud-free imagery over a given area.
