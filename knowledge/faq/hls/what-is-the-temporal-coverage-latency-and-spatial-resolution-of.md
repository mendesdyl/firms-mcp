- Temporal coverage: Landsat HLS product data in FIRMS extend from April 11, 2013, to the present. Sentinel-2 HLS product data extend from November 28, 2015, to the present.
- Temporal resolution: Landsat 8/9 products have a temporal resolution of 8 days, while Sentinel-2A/2B/2C products have a temporal resolution of 4 to 5 days.
- Data latency: Data is typically available in FIRMS approximately 2 to 4 days after the satellite observation. Please note that NASA tentatively plans to make HLS data available approximately 6 hours after satellite observation in the near future.
- Spatial resolution: All output HLS products (including L30 for Landsat and S30 for Sentinel-2) are maintained or resampled to a spatial resolution of 30 meters.
