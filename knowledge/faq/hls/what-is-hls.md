HLS stands for the Harmonized Landsat Sentinel-2 dataset. It provides 30-meter spatial resolution imagery derived from the Operational Land Imager (OLI) aboard the Landsat 8 and 9 satellites and from the Multi-Spectral Instrument (MSI) aboard the Sentinel-2A, 2B, and 2C satellites. By combining observations from these satellites, HLS provides high-quality, continuous observations of Earth's surface at 30-meter spatial resolution approximately every 1.6 days. For more information see the FIRMS blog on [Adding HLS False Color Composite Layer to FIRMS](https://www.earthdata.nasa.gov/news/feature-articles/new-hls-false-color-composite-layer-added-firms) and also information on the [HLS project](https://www.earthdata.nasa.gov/data/projects/hls).
