The status categories in the Steel Plants layer are:

- Announced: Projects that have been announced in corporate or governmental planning documents, but have not begun operation.
- Construction: Physical plant structure building has begun.
- Operating: Plant is operating one or more iron or steelmaking furnace(s).
- Operating pre-retirement: Plant is operating one or more iron or steelmaking furnace(s).
- Mothballed: Plant iron and steelmaking has been idled such that it cannot be brought into operation immediately, but is not closed.
- Retired: Plant has ceased operations and no longer has ability to produce iron and/or steel.
- Cancelled: Plant previously planned or under development that has been cancelled. If no progress or announcements for a proposed plant are made after 5 years, the plant is considered to be cancelled.
