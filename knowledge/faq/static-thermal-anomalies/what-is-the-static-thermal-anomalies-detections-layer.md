This layer consists of MODIS, VIIRS, and Landsat active fire detections that intersect with, or are adjacent, to areas identified in the Static Thermal Anomalies - Mask layer for the date/time range selected by the user. The date/time range of the active fire detections displayed will be coincident with the date/time range selected by the user.
