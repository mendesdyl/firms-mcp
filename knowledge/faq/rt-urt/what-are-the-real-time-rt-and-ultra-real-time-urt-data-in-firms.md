NASA EOSDIS defines real-time (RT) data as data that are available to the end user within one hour of the satellite fly-over (see [Data Latency](https://www.earthdata.nasa.gov/learn/earth-observation-data-basics/data-latency) and [What time does the satellite pass over my area](https://www.earthdata.nasa.gov/data/tools/firms/faq#heading-accordion-124369-8)?). Ultra real-time (URT) data are available much quicker—within minutes. Within FIRMS, the URT data are available within a minute of satellite overpass for select direct readout stations in the U.S. and Canada.
