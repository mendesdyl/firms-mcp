NASA's Fire Information for Resource Management System ([FIRMS](https://www.earthdata.nasa.gov/data/tools/firms)) provides access to near real-time data related to wildfire activity. Learn more by reading the most frequently asked questions about FIRMS along with answers and links to resources provided by our experts.

#### Share your questions, comments, or impact stories

Get in touch by emailing Earthdata Support at [earthdata-support@nasa.gov](mailto:earthdata-support@nasa.gov) with FIRMS in the subject line.

#### Join our FIRMS email listserv

Get updates on new blogs, trainings and information on data outages. To subscribe: e-mail [lance-firms-users-join@lists.nasa.gov](mailto:lance-firms-users-join@lists.nasa.gov) (no subject or text is required).

Warning: Super-heated smoke plumes can cause artifacts in nighttime detections. See: [Super heated smoke plumes cause data artifacts in nighttime detections](https://landweb.modaps.eosdis.nasa.gov/displayissue?id=339) (July 17, 2020). Please also see "[Are there ever false detections or data artifacts](https://www.earthdata.nasa.gov/data/tools/firms/faq#heading-accordion-124439-1)?" below, under the FAQ section "[Caveats to consider when using active fire data from FIRMS](https://www.earthdata.nasa.gov/data/tools/firms/faq#caveats)."
