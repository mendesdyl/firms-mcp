- MODIS Terra are available from 1 November 2000 to the present
- MODIS Aqua from July 2002 to the present.
- VIIRS S-NPP data are available from 20 January 2012 to November 2, 2026
- VIIRS NOAA-20 data are available from 1 April 2018 to the present
- VIIRS NOAA-21 are available from 17 January 2024 to the present.
