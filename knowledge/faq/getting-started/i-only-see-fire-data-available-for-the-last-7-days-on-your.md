- [Data for the last 2 months](https://firms.modaps.eosdis.nasa.gov/active_fire/) can be downloaded as text (TXT) files.
- Older data can be obtained through the [FIRMS Fire Archive Download](https://firms.modaps.eosdis.nasa.gov/download/)
