[Data for the last 2 months](https://firms.modaps.eosdis.nasa.gov/active_fire/) can be downloaded as text (TXT) files older data can be obtained through the [MODIS Fire Archive Download Tool](https://firms.modaps.eosdis.nasa.gov/download/) or directly from the [MODIS Fire Team Science Computing Facility at the University of Maryland](http://modis-fire.umd.edu/af.html). MODIS data are available from November 2000 (for Terra) and from July 2002 (for Aqua) to the present. VIIRS 375 m data are available from 20 January 2012 to the present (for Suomi NPP) and 1 January 2020 to the present (for NOAA-20), and 17 January 2024 to the present (for NOAA-21).
