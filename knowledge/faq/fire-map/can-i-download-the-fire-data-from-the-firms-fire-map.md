Active fire data are not currently available for download directly from the FIRMS Fire Maps. However, active fire locations from MODIS and VIIRS (and Landsat for the US and Canada) are available for download for the last 24 hours, last 48 hours, and last 7 days on the [FIRMS](https://www.earthdata.nasa.gov/data/tools/firms) page. Older MODIS and VIIRS data can be obtained through the [Archive Download Tool](https://firms.modaps.eosdis.nasa.gov/download/).
