The OMPS instruments aboard the Suomi NPP and NOAA-20 platforms observe Earth at approximately the same time on a daily basis (roughly within 1 hour of each other). However, the OMPS instrument aboard NOAA-20 has improved spatial resolution, 17 km x 13 km, compared to the 50 km x 50 km resolution of Suomi NPP OMPS. So the NOAA-20 OMPS AI provides much finer resolution compared to the Suomi NPP OMPS AI, allowing for better detection and monitoring of smoke and aerosols.
